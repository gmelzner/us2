What is Mental Load?

🧠

The Invisible Work

Mental load is the cognitive labor of managing a household: remembering appointments, planning meals, tracking supplies, coordinating schedules. It's exhausting work that often goes unseen.

⚖️

Often Unbalanced

Research shows mental load is often unevenly distributed in relationships. This can lead to burnout, resentment, and conflict. Awareness is the first step to balance.
