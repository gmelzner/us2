What Is a Mental Load Test?

Mental load is the invisible work of managing a household: not just doing the tasks, but remembering, planning, coordinating, and anticipating everything that needs to be done. A mental load test measures how each partner perceives that distribution.

The problem is that this work is invisible. It doesn't leave dirty dishes or clothes hanging. That's why it's so hard to talk about without it turning into an argument. A test puts numbers and charts where there used to be only feelings.

Why Doing It Together Changes the Conversation

Arguing about who does more is exhausting and never leads anywhere. The test changes the dynamic because it turns feelings into concrete data. It's not "I feel like I do everything" vs. "that's not true." It's a chart that both of you can see and analyze without getting defensive.

💡 Couples who retake the test every month or two can track their evolution: the chart shows whether the gaps are closing or not.
